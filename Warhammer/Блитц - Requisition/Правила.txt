Summary: Fast-play variant that heavily impacts Setup
The arrival of the Herakon Cluster was neither a secret nor
an unexpected phenomena. Eldar prophecy, astropath
intuition, and the bidding of Dark Gods have all foretold of
its arrival. For many decades have the forces arraigned
before the system plotted their conquest, and now that
warpstorms have parted there will be no resource left in
reserve. Bring the full might of your people to bear and seize
the Herakon Cluster in haste, for the hour is late and doom is
nigh!
During setup, after players have gathered their Initial Forces
and have chosen their System Tiles but before any tile has
been deployed players will draft Requisitions in the
following manner, starting with the First Player:
- Choose a suit from among those unchosen: Authority,
Reconnaisance, or Command
- Shuffle all Requisitions belonging to that suit (6) and deal
one greater than the number of players to yourself,
discarding the rest to the box [Example: in a 3-player game
you would deal 4 Requisitions]
- Choose 1 from among those Requisitions dealt to keep and
pass the remaining Requisitions clockwise to the next player
- Once all Requisitions of that suit have been chosen, discard
the remaining unchosen Requisition to the box
- The next player in turn Order [Example: the Second Player
during the second round of drafting] will then repeat this
process, beginning at the first step
- Do not reveal any Requisitions, both those chosen by
players and those discarded to the box, during the draft
Once all Requisition suits have been drafted, proceed to
setup as normal, with one exception
- When players place a System Tile during Setup, they must
also reveal and resolve a Requisition card. If a Requisition
card cannot be resolved when it is revealed, it is discarded
without effect
Additionally, when playing with Requisitions, limit the
maximum number of turns of the game from 8 to 5.
Requisition cards will grant players additional Forces,
resources, abilities, or other effects at the start of the game,
often restricted or limited by the effects of the Requisition
card. Requisition cards will drastically accelerate the pacing
and stakes of the game.
Requisition cards are not secret, but are treated as hidden
information during the course of the draft and are
incrementally revealed during setup. This is to allow players
greater tactical flexibility in deploying certain Requisitions
and a greater level of interactivity during setup.
