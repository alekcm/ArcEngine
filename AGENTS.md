# Art direction for expansion creatures

When creating or replacing artwork featuring creatures from a Nemesis expansion, depict the creatures from that specific expansion, not the base-game Intruders or generic monsters. Use the expansion's original card sheets and miniatures as references for anatomy and silhouette; use base-game art only as a reference for visual style. For Carnomorphs, preserve their recognizable asymmetric, flesh-and-bone anatomy from the Carnomorph DLC rather than substituting xenomorph-like, insectoid, bat-like, or generic skeletal monsters. For Voidseeders (Кошмары), follow the original DLC cards and miniatures, and do not add Cthulhu-like faces or dangling facial tentacles. For Chytrids (Хитриды), depict one vast interconnected fungal organism spreading throughout the ship, not separate independently acting creatures. The offshoot, deathcap and queen are parts of the same organism, visibly joined to continuous mycelium across floors and walls into the ship. Depict the mycelium as the same continuous, dimensional fungal tissue as its offshoots and queen, built from varied fungal growths, protrusions, spikes and pseudopods across the ship, not as a shapeless rough dusty coating, thick vines, plant roots, slime, or mucus. The user approved Chytrid Pseudopod and Spikes (chyAttack17.jpg and chyAttack19.jpg), and the later generated attacks chyAttack1, 4, 10, 12, 14 and events chyEvent1–4, including their angular green growths. Preserve these accepted arts; use their morphology and style as guidance, but never reproduce their composition or background in other card art. Passing a full approved card as an image reference previously caused near-duplicates: inspect carefully and reject copied scenes. Integrate the queen into the infested ship rather than staging her as a standalone dungeon boss. Preserve the individual model silhouettes, but do not treat the separate tabletop miniatures as independent organisms. Use green fungi and spores; yellow may be used together with green, but do not depict pink creatures, frog-like aliens, or generic animals. Check the connection, colour and fungal anatomy in each generated image. Generate one image with one request per card name, never multiple candidates or separate generations for copies of that name; report a failed or unsuitable result rather than silently retrying it.

Before installing generated artwork, visually inspect the actual output against the relevant expansion references and the card's effect. Reject uncertain or mismatched creature designs rather than claiming they are accurate. For Chytrids, use one illustration per card name/effect, shared by copies of that card even when their values differ; do not generate separate art for those copies. For other expansions, each card needs its own distinct illustration, even where original physical cards reuse artwork; do not copy one generated picture to multiple cards. Do not revert rejected artwork to the original images: generate suitable replacements instead. If generation fails or no suitable replacement is available, leave the existing file unchanged and explicitly disclose remaining duplicates and incomplete work. Report generation successes, rejected outputs, and failures separately; automated tests cannot establish visual accuracy.
