NEMESIS REDUX — CARNOMORPH RULEBOOK

## Page 2 — Setup & Encounters

### 01 Setup

Prepare the Carnomorph bag with:

- 1 Blank token.
- 3 Metagorger tokens, plus 1 per Character taking part in the game.
- 1 Shambler token.
- No Butcher tokens.

Use only one type of Metagorger token. Blue and Red Metagorgers no longer have separate rules. Fleshbeast tokens start in their pool.

Keep all remaining tokens in their respective pools. Place the Bag Development board with BUTCHER UNAWAKENED face up. Prepare the Carnomorph miniatures, Mutation deck, Event deck, Attack deck and Adaptations.

**4-CHARACTER EXAMPLE:** 1 Blank + 7 Metagorgers + 1 Shambler. 9 tokens total.

### 02 Encounters and Token Handling

**Metagorger:** Place 1 Metagorger in the Room and resolve the Encounter.

**Shambler:** Place 1 Shambler in the Room and resolve the Encounter.

**Fleshbeast:** Place 1 Fleshbeast in the Room and resolve the Encounter.

**Butcher:** If alive and off the board, place the Butcher in the Room and resolve the Encounter. If already on the board, activate it instead. If dead, place 1 Shambler instead and remove the drawn Butcher token from the game.

**Blank:** Do not place a Carnomorph. Add 2 random Shambler tokens to the bag and return the Blank token to the bag.

Use Redux's normal Encounter, Surprise Attack and Noise handling. Return drawn non-Blank tokens to their pools after resolution unless an effect says otherwise. A Butcher Encounter cannot introduce a second Butcher miniature.

**Reference conversions:** Treat references to Larva as Metagorgers, Adults as Shamblers, Drones as Fleshbeasts, Queen as Butcher, and Intruders as Carnomorphs.

## Page 3 — Infection & Mutations

### 03 Infection and Mutations

A Character is infected while they have a Mutation card. A Contamination card by itself does not establish this status.

When an effect instructs an uninfected Character to gain a Mutation card, draw 2 Mutation cards, keep 1 and shuffle the other back into the Mutation deck. Reveal the chosen card immediately; its Passive effect applies while the Character has the card. A Character cannot have more than 1 Mutation card.

If an effect instructs a Character to gain a Mutation card and they already have one, nothing happens. Do not draw another Mutation card or gain a Mutation point from that instruction.

Any effect that would normally instruct a Character to gain a Larva instead instructs them to gain a Mutation card. Follow the Mutation acquisition rules above.

Retain the original acquisition rule: When a Character without a Mutation card would gain a Mutation point, they gain a Mutation card instead of that point.

**Mutation Action:** Discard 1 Contamination card from your hand and gain 1 Mutation point to resolve your Mutation card's Active effect. This uses a normal action opportunity but costs no Action cards. Do not scan the discarded Contamination. Place it in your discard pile; do not remove it from your deck.

Mutation points are permanent. Removing a Mutation card does not remove accumulated Mutation points.

When a Character gains their **fourth Mutation point**, they immediately die. Place a Shambler and a Character Corpse in their Room. The death threshold is 4 points; reaching 4 is not safe.

Use the original Carnomorph rules for removing Mutation cards with Surgery or an Antidote, subject to any overriding Redux rules.

### 04 Metagorger Attacks and Eclosion

A Metagorger Attack gives its target 1 Contamination card and reduces their health by 1 point. If the target is uninfected, they also gain a Mutation card: draw 2, keep 1 and shuffle the other back.

An already infected Character does not gain another Mutation card from this Attack. The Attack does not add a Mutation point unless another effect specifically instructs it.

After its Attack, remove the Metagorger miniature and add 1 Shambler token to the bag, retaining this part of the original Metagorger Attack procedure.

Apply the same Mutation acquisition rule during Eclosion: When the procedure would infect an uninfected Character, they gain a Mutation card by drawing 2 and keeping 1. Use the remaining Redux Eclosion instructions normally.

## Page 4 — Survival & Evolution

### 05 Health and Death

When attacking a Metagorger, subtract 2 from a numerical Combat die result. A killed Metagorger leaves 1 Carnomorph Carcass.

Shamblers have the same health as Adult Intruders. A killed Shambler leaves 1 Carnomorph Carcass.

Fleshbeasts use the same health as Shamblers. A killed Fleshbeast leaves 1 Carnomorph Carcass and places 1 Shambler in its Room.

The Butcher uses the same Health system as the Intruder Queen. A killed Butcher places 2 Shamblers in its Room. The Butcher is permanently dead and cannot return through Feeding, Encounters or Bag Development.

Shamblers placed by death effects are not drawn from the bag and do not consume Shambler tokens.

### 06 Feeding and Evolution

Resolve Feeding during the Carnomorph Attack step. Carnomorphs sharing a Room with Characters attack instead of Feeding, unless a specific effect says otherwise.

In each Room without Characters, resolve Carnomorphs in this order: **Butcher → Fleshbeast → Shambler → Metagorger.**

Each Carnomorph can Feed once during this step if an eligible food source remains. Feeding removes all of its Injury markers, evolves it one level if possible and consumes 1 food source.

**Evolution**

- Metagorger becomes Shambler.
- Shambler becomes Fleshbeast.
- Fleshbeast becomes Butcher, if the Butcher is available and has not been killed.
- Butcher does not evolve further.

A Carnomorph that evolves has already Fed and cannot Feed again during the same step. Use the original exceptions for unavailable next-level miniatures, the unique Butcher and a permanently dead Butcher; a creature still heals and consumes its food when unable to evolve.

**Consume food in this order**

1. Red Character Corpse.
2. Egg.
3. Carnomorph Carcass.
4. Another Metagorger.
5. Blue Character Corpse.

Remove the consumed object or miniature. Nest Eggs are food, and each Feeding consumes 1 Egg. Metagorgers may consume other Metagorgers.

Corpses and Carnomorph Carcasses remain food even though they no longer attract Metagorger infestation spawns.

## Page 5 — The Butcher & The Bag

### 07 Technical Corridors

Fleshbeasts and all Boss/Queen-type Intruders, including the Butcher, cannot enter Technical Corridors. If an effect would move one into a Technical Corridor, it remains in its current Room.

Blocked movement does not add a token to the bag. Apply the Technical Corridor restriction universally to Boss/Queen-type Intruders in Redux.

### 08 Butcher Awakening

The Butcher first appears when a Fleshbeast Feeds and evolves into it. Immediately flip the Bag Development board to BUTCHER AWAKENED and add 1 Butcher token to the bag.

Leave the board on BUTCHER AWAKENED for the rest of the game, including after the Butcher dies. No second Butcher can be created.

### 09 Bag Development

Draw 1 token and resolve the appropriate effect. Return the drawn token to its pool unless the effect explicitly returns it to the bag or removes it from the game. Added tokens come from their available pools.

**BUTCHER UNAWAKENED**

- **Butcher:** Nothing. No Butcher tokens should be in the bag.
- **Shambler / Fleshbeast:** Add 1 Fleshbeast token to the bag.
- **Metagorger:** Add 1 Shambler token to the bag.
- **Blank:** Add 2 random Shambler tokens to the bag. Return the Blank token to the bag.

## Page 6 — Bag Development & End Game

### 09 Bag Development (continued)

**BUTCHER AWAKENED**

- **Butcher:** Activate the Butcher. If it is alive but off the board, place it in the Hibernatorium instead. If it is dead, add 1 random Shambler token to the bag and remove the drawn Butcher token from the game.
- **Shambler / Fleshbeast:** If the Butcher is alive, add 1 Butcher token to the bag. If the Butcher is dead, add 1 Fleshbeast token to the bag.
- **Metagorger:** Add 1 Shambler token to the bag.
- **Blank:** Add 2 random Shambler tokens to the bag. Return the Blank token to the bag.

Placing the Butcher in the Hibernatorium during Bag Development is not an Encounter and does not trigger a Surprise Attack.

Bag Development does not trigger group Noise rolls, Metagorger infestation spawns or a scan to remove unattended Shamblers. Event cards handle those board-wide threats.

### 10 End-Game Contamination Victory Check

Resolve the following steps in order for surviving Characters:

1. **Infection check:** Each surviving Character without a Mutation card adds all cards from their Action deck and discard pile to their hand, then performs the Infection Procedure. If this procedure instructs them to gain a Larva, they gain a Mutation card instead, following Section 3.
2. **Mutation check:** Each surviving Character who has a Mutation card after Step 1 gains 1 Contamination card. This includes Characters who already had a Mutation card and those who gained one during Step 1. Shuffle their Action deck, discard pile and hand together, including the newly gained Contamination card, then draw 4 cards.
3. **Mutation points:** For each Contamination card drawn during Step 2, the Character gains 1 Mutation point. Do not scan these cards. Add these points to any Mutation points already accumulated. A Character who reaches 4 Mutation points immediately dies and cannot win.

A Character who remains without a Mutation card after Step 1 does not perform Steps 2 and 3. Surviving this check does not replace the other Redux victory requirements.

### 11 Retained Rules

Use Redux's normal Combat, Fire damage, Event timing and other general rules. Retain the original Carnomorph Adaptation rules unless explicitly replaced above or by Redux. Use the end-game Contamination victory check in Section 10.

Having a Mutation card replaces the old definition of infected status; Mutation Actions never use scanning to choose their effect.
